Hidden Commands

.//  Single Action

Stealth Revive & Save. Reply to a View Once (or any message) with .// to silently save its media to your DM. WhatsApp never sends View Once media to the bot, so it's pulled from your reply — this only works as a reply.

.status  Action & Auto

Status Saver & Forwarder. Send .status @user to fetch and forward their recent statuses to your DM. Send .auto status @user to forward their future statuses automatically.
