Hidden Commands

.// (Single Action) — Stealth Revive & Save

Reply to any View Once message or normal message with .// to instantly forward the media to your personal DM.

./// or .stealth (Toggle) — Targeted Auto-Stealth

Send ./// in any chat to toggle Auto-Stealth for that specific chat. Or use .stealth @user to target a specific person. When ON, every incoming View Once message from them is instantly downloaded and silently forwarded to your DM. Use .stealth all to toggle globally.

.status (Action & Auto) — Status Saver & Forwarder

Send .status @user to instantly fetch and forward all of their recently posted statuses to your DM. Send .auto status @user to automatically forward their future statuses as soon as they are posted.
